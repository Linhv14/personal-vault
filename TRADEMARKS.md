# 7key Trademark Guidelines

"7key", "7KEY" and the 7key logo are trademarks of linhv14. The code is open source; the
name and logo are not. These guidelines say how you may use them.

## You may, without asking

- Say truthfully that your product works with 7key, or that it is based on 7key's open
  source code, for example "compatible with 7key" or "a fork of 7key".
- Use the name in articles, reviews, tutorials and talks about 7key.
- Link to https://7key.online.

## You need permission to

- Use "7key" or the logo in the name, domain, logo or app store listing of your own product,
  service or fork.
- Run a hosted service that presents itself as 7key or could be mistaken for the official
  one.
- Sell merchandise with the name or logo.

## Never

- Suggest that your product is made, endorsed or certified by 7key when it is not.
- Use a confusingly similar name or logo.

If you publish a modified version, give it a different name and remove the 7key logo.
