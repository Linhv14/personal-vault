# Code of conduct

7key adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. This page summarises it; the linked text is the one that applies.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn
  from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use 7key.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code to **security@7key.online** with
"Conduct" in the subject. Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide on a response in proportion to the harm, following the
Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban.
